# Launch checklist

The site is technically ready for a preview deploy, not for indexed lead generation. Keep `PUBLIC_INDEX_SITE=false` and leave `PUBLIC_FORMSPREE_ENDPOINT` empty until every applicable item below is evidenced and approved.

## Business identity and legal pages

- [ ] Confirm the responsible legal entity, legal form and authorised representative.
- [ ] Confirm a valid service address and public contact details.
- [ ] Confirm register, VAT and professional disclosures that actually apply.
- [ ] Replace the draft `Impressum` and privacy copy with reviewed final information.
- [ ] Complete a formal trademark and domain clearance for the working name `IsarKlima`.

## Regulated work and operating scope

- [ ] Confirm the applicable Handwerksrolle or cross-border service position with the relevant chamber.
- [ ] Verify personal competency certificates for the refrigerants and systems that will be handled.
- [ ] Verify any required business certification and retain the evidence.
- [ ] Confirm professional/business insurance and responsible technical personnel.
- [ ] Define which fixed electrical work is included and the registered electrical party responsible for it.
- [ ] Confirm systems, refrigerants, languages, logistics and service radius actually supported.
- [ ] Review every public claim against that evidence before removing the preview banner.

## Enquiries and data protection

- [ ] Create the dedicated Formspree form and add the verified endpoint in Netlify.
- [ ] Confirm controller details, purpose, legal basis, recipients, transfers and retention.
- [ ] Complete the required Formspree data-processing terms and privacy disclosure.
- [ ] Test success, validation, spam handling and notification routing without using real customer data.
- [ ] Confirm the exact quotation process and response ownership.

## Search and public release

- [ ] Set and verify the final production domain in `PUBLIC_SITE_URL`.
- [ ] Verify canonical, hreflang, sitemap, robots and redirects on the final domain.
- [ ] Change `PUBLIC_INDEX_SITE` to `true` only under explicit launch approval.
- [ ] Rebuild and confirm `index, follow` plus `Allow: /` publicly.
- [ ] Add analytics or external embeds only when genuinely required and reflected in consent/privacy controls.
- [ ] Create and verify a Google Business Profile only for a real eligible operating setup; do not use virtual or invented locations.
